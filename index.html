The science

This is not what a smell objectively looks like.

Nothing does — smell has no inherent colour. What research does show is that most people, synesthete or not, match smells to colours, shapes and sounds in surprisingly consistent ways. OSMOS is built on those shared crossmodal correspondences, and every visual choice below is labelled by how strong its basis is:

Evidence backed by published studies   Physical follows from the chemistry   Artistic a design choice, not a finding

True olfactory–visual synesthesia is different: it's rare, and each synesthete's visions are personal — one person's chocolate is pink and stripy, another's is a hazy mist (Russell et al. 2015). OSMOS doesn't claim to show anyone's synesthesia; it shows the patterns most minds share.

Where the numbers come from

Each scent is described with the 20 semantic descriptors used by Keller & Vosshall (2016) — the same vocabulary behind the DREAM olfaction prediction challenge — plus earthy and metallic from the Dravnieks (1985) odour atlas, and ratings of intensity, pleasantness and volatility.

Be clear-eyed about what that means: those studies rated single molecules with real panels. OSMOS's values for everyday smells like "coffee" are editorial estimates for a typical encounter, informed by that work and by flavour chemistry — not measurements. Pleasantness especially varies by person and culture.

What OSMOS can't tell you

It can't smell. The camera recognises objects (a cup, a book), not odours — and each object's scent is an assumption, shown as one. Intensity depends heavily on concentration and distance. Colour matches shift between cultures. And many people are blind to specific molecules (musks, β-ionone, rotundone), so the "same" smell genuinely differs from nose to nose.

Model settings
Model adaptation
Active scents gradually fade, as they would in your nose. Remove a scent and give it a minute to recover. Hold on the stage to sniff.
Sound layer
A drone per scent: pitch from the odour–pitch research, timbre from shape.
